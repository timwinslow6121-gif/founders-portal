PLAN CARD TAB - setup instructions
A 2026 vs 2027 comparison card, like AJ's charts, inside the spreadsheet.
Pick a carrier and a plan from dropdowns; the card fills itself in.

Takes about 5 minutes. No scripts needed.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 - make the tab
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Add a new tab. Name it exactly:   Plan Card

-------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 - type these labels
-------------------------------------------------------------------
A1:   Carrier
A2:   Plan
A4:   Benefit
B4:   2026
C4:   2027

Then in A5 going down, type the benefit names in this order.
These match AJ's chart layout. Skip any you don't care about.

  Premium
  Part B giveback
  Medical deductible
  Max out-of-pocket
  PCP
  Specialist
  Referrals
  Inpatient hospital
  Outpatient hospital
  Outpatient surgery (ASC)
  Emergency room
  Urgent care
  Ambulance (ground)
  Lab services
  Rx deductible
  Rx retail (30-day)
  Dental - allowance
  Vision - eyeglasses
  Hearing aids
  OTC allowance
  Fitness
  Service area
  Key notes

(That is A5 through A27.)

-------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 - carrier dropdown in B1
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Click B1. Data > Data validation > Add rule.
  Criteria: "Dropdown (from a range)"
  Range:    put this in the box, including the quotes-free text:

Aetna,BCBS,Devoted,Healthspring,Humana,UHC

  (Use "Dropdown" and type those six as separate items if the
  range option is fiddly.)

Pick Aetna to start.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 - plan dropdown in B2
-------------------------------------------------------------------
This one lists only the plans belonging to the carrier in B1.

First, park the plan list somewhere out of the way.
Click F1 and paste this formula:

=UNIQUE(INDIRECT($B$1&"!B2:B"))

That gives every plan name for the chosen carrier.

Now click B2. Data > Data validation > Add rule.
  Criteria: Dropdown (from a range)
  Range:    'Plan Card'!F1:F200

Pick a plan.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5 - the two formulas that do all the work
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Click B5 and paste this:

=IFERROR(INDEX(INDIRECT($B$1&"!E:E"),MATCH(1,(INDIRECT($B$1&"!B:B")=$B$2)*(INDIRECT($B$1&"!C:C")=B$4)*(INDIRECT($B$1&"!D:D")=$A5),0)),"")

Press Ctrl+Shift+Enter if it doesn't work on plain Enter.

Then click C5 and paste the SAME formula (it reads C4 = 2027
automatically because of the B$4 reference):

=IFERROR(INDEX(INDIRECT($B$1&"!E:E"),MATCH(1,(INDIRECT($B$1&"!B:B")=$B$2)*(INDIRECT($B$1&"!C:C")=C$4)*(INDIRECT($B$1&"!D:D")=$A5),0)),"")

Now select B5:C5, copy, and paste down to B27:C27.

Done. Change the dropdowns and the card refills.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE FORMULA IS DOING
-------------------------------------------------------------------
INDIRECT($B$1&"!E:E")  - look in the tab named in B1 (the carrier),
                         column E, which is Value.
MATCH(1, (...)*(...)*(...), 0)
                       - find the row where plan name AND year AND
                         benefit all match. Multiplying the three
                         TRUE/FALSE tests together is how you do a
                         3-condition lookup without a helper column.
IFERROR(..., "")       - leave the cell blank if there is no such row.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
READING IT
-------------------------------------------------------------------
A blank cell means that benefit is NOT IN that year's source data.
It does NOT mean $0 and it does NOT mean the benefit was removed.
2026 came from CMS with deep clinical detail; most 2027 came from
carrier first look sheets, which are shorter. The benefit lists differ.

Aetna 2027 is CMS-approved. BCBS, Devoted, UHC, Humana and
Healthspring 2027 are first look and can still change.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
IF YOU WANT IT PRETTIER
-------------------------------------------------------------------
- Bold row 4, freeze rows 1-4 (View > Freeze > Up to row 4)
- Widen B and C to about 300px, and turn on text wrapping
- Hide column F (the plan list) - right-click the column > Hide
- Format > Alternating colours on A4:C27
