AEP 2025 — my actual call log
I ran AEP on a VOIP system that auto-texted a booking link to every missed call. No other agent had that — they used a personal cell, or the pharmacy took messages. I also have the smallest book, so the fewest calls.
51% went unanswered
310 of 617 calls — every working day for nine weeks. 40% of those people had to be missed more than once: 66 customers, 211 failed attempts, one tried 13 times before we connected. On top of that, 861 text messages with 164 people. 1,478 separate touchpoints in nine weeks, one agent, and no record of what any of them decided.
And that is only what I can see. It excludes every call and text on personal cells and everything through the pharmacy line. This is the floor, measured on the one channel that kept records — and the agents with bigger books and no system had it worse.
What already worked — and why I'm confident
Anyone who left a voicemail got an automatic text with a link to my calendar. They picked a time themselves, from my real availability, without either of us making another call.
79% self-booked
191 of 242 AEP appointments were booked by the customer, not by me — 156 kept appointments I never had to schedule. That was one crude automation on one channel. The portal does this deliberately and for every agent: reach customers before they call, let the ones who are fine stay home, and keep the calendar for the ones who aren't.
