Why it's worth it — what the system has already caught

  • Commission errors that balanced perfectly and were invisible. UHC bundles its Part D override into some payments; the portal was reading those as a single agent commission. Totals still tied to the penny, so nothing looked wrong — mis-handled since May. Found, fixed, and a guard added so unrecognized amounts now stop for review instead of paying out silently.
  • Recurring manual work, eliminated. AJ had been hand-splitting those same rows every month — 26 May, 4 June, 3 July. Now automatic.
  • Repeated overpayments, and the cause fixed. Two incidents (38 rows, 1 Aug; 16 rows, 3 Aug) traced to one root cause: the edit screen didn't show the agent's contract rate, so a correct rate was being overwritten. It now shows the rate and blocks off-contract saves.
  • Rolled-up business made visible. One month of UHC alone: 121 transactions, $3,173 of retired-agent business, $1,587 of it yours — previously not recorded anywhere.
  • Traceability restored. 509 commission payments had lost their link to the customer they belonged to. Reconnected, with a safety check that refuses any link whose name doesn't match — which caught a genuine mis-match and flagged it for a human.
  • The book made trustworthy. Carrier-by-carrier reconciliation against the authoritative books of business, duplicate records collapsed, and CMS plan data corrected — including 82 plans wrongly labeled as having no drug coverage.
